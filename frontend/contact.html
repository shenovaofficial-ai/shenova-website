Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days across India. Express delivery (2–3 days) is available at checkout for select pin codes. All orders above ₹2,000 ship for free.
We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached. To initiate a return, email us with your order number.
