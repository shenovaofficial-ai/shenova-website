Email Support
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days across India. Express delivery (2–3 days) is available at checkout for select pin codes. All orders above ₹2,000 ship for free.
What is the return and exchange policy?
We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached. To initiate a return, email us at shenovaofficial@gmail.com with your order number.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. You can also WhatsApp us at +91 70417 02391 with your order number for real-time updates.
Can I modify or cancel my order?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order enters our fulfilment process and cannot be changed. Please contact us immediately if you need to make any changes.
Do you offer custom sizing or alterations?
We currently offer standard sizes as listed on each product page. For bespoke or alteration inquiries, please email us directly and our team will be happy to discuss options for you.
