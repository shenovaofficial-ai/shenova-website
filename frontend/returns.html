01 · Return Eligibility

Is your item
eligible?

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions. Items that do not meet these criteria may be returned to you at your cost.

Within 14 days — Return request raised within 14 days of the delivery date.
Unworn & unwashed — Item has not been worn, washed, or altered in any way.
Original tags attached — All brand tags, price tags, and labels are intact and attached.
Original packaging — Returned in the original Shenova packaging or a clean equivalent.
Not a final-sale item — Items marked "Final Sale" or purchased during clearance events are not eligible.
Not innerwear or accessories — For hygiene reasons, innerwear, swimwear, and earrings cannot be returned.

03 · Exchanges

Need a different
size or colour?

We are happy to exchange your item for a different size or colour, subject to availability. If the desired variant is not in stock, we will offer you store credit or a full refund.

Exchange requests must be made within 14 days of delivery, and the original item must meet all return eligibility criteria above.

Size Exchange
Swap for a different size free of charge. Subject to availability. Processed within 5–7 days after pickup.
Colour Exchange
Prefer another colour? We'll swap it if stock is available. Otherwise, a full refund will be issued.
04 · Refunds

Getting your
money back.

Once your return is approved and received, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

Please note that your bank may take an additional 2–5 business days to reflect the amount in your account, depending on your payment provider.

Payment Method Refund Mode Timeline Eligible
UPI / Net Banking Original source 5–7 business days Yes
Credit / Debit Card Original card 7–10 business days Yes
Cash on Delivery Bank transfer 5–7 business days Yes
Coupon / Discount Store credit 1–2 business days Partial
Final Sale Items — — No
Shipping charges are non-refundable Original shipping fees (if any) will not be included in the refund. However, return pickup is always arranged free of charge by Shenova.
05 · Non-Returnable Items

Items we
cannot accept.

The following items are strictly non-returnable due to hygiene, safety, or policy reasons. Please review these carefully before placing your order.

Innerwear & intimate apparel — Cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, regardless of condition.
Earrings & pierced jewellery — Cannot be returned once removed from sealed packaging.
Final Sale & clearance items — Items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase are not eligible for return or exchange.
Worn, washed, or altered items — Any item showing signs of use, washing, or alteration will not be accepted.
Items returned after 14 days — Return window expires 14 days after the confirmed delivery date.
