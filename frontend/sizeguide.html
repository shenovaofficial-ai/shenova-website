02 · Measuring Guide

How to
measure yourself.

Use a soft measuring tape and measure over lightweight clothing or directly on skin for the most accurate result.

Bust
Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
Waist
Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist, usually just above the belly button.
Hips
Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, about 8 inches below your natural waist.
03 · Fit Tips

Tips for the
best fit.

A few things to keep in mind when choosing your size.

01
Always measure before ordering. Sizes vary between styles. Even if you know your size, take measurements for each new style — our fitted dresses and flowy cuts may fit differently.
02
Between two sizes? Go up. Shenova pieces are designed with a slightly tailored fit. If your measurements fall between sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size for comfort.
03
Use a soft tape measure. A metal ruler or a string approximation won't give accurate results. Borrow a soft tailor's tape or use a piece of string and then measure it against a ruler.
04
Free size exchange. If your item doesn't fit as expected, we offer a free size exchange within 14 days of delivery. See our Returns Policy for details.
