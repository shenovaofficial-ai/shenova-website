01 · Fabric Care

Care by
fabric type.

Different fabrics need different care. Here's exactly how to treat the most common fabrics used in Shenova garments.

Cotton
Cotton & Cotton Blends
✦ Hand wash or machine wash on gentle cycle at 30°C
✦ Use mild detergent — no harsh chemicals
✦ Do not wring — gently squeeze out excess water
✦ Dry in shade, flat or on a hanger
✦ Iron on medium heat while slightly damp
Chiffon
Chiffon & Georgette
✦ Hand wash only in cold water — very gentle
✦ Use a delicate fabric detergent or baby shampoo
✦ Never wring or twist — roll in a towel to absorb water
✦ Hang dry away from direct sunlight
✦ Iron on lowest setting with a pressing cloth
Silk
Silk & Satin
✦ Dry clean recommended for pure silk
✦ If hand washing, use cold water with silk-specific detergent
✦ Never soak for more than 5 minutes
✦ Dry flat on a clean towel, out of sunlight
✦ Iron on the lowest setting, always inside-out
Rayon
Rayon & Viscose
✦ Hand wash in cold water or dry clean
✦ Rayon shrinks — never use hot water or a dryer
✦ Gently squeeze, do not wring
✦ Hang dry or lay flat — keep away from direct sun
✦ Iron on low heat while slightly damp
02 · Do's & Don'ts

What to do
& what to avoid.

A quick reference for keeping your Shenova pieces in perfect condition.

Do's
Wash dark colours separately the first time
Turn garments inside out before washing
Use a mesh laundry bag for delicates
Store folded or hung in a cool dry place
Treat stains immediately and gently
Air out garments after wearing before storing
Don'ts
Never use bleach or strong detergents
Don't tumble dry any Shenova piece
Avoid soaking for extended periods
Don't dry in direct sunlight — causes fading
Don't wring or twist delicate fabrics
Never iron directly on embellishments
03 · Washing

Washing
your garment.

For most Shenova pieces, gentle hand washing is the best way to preserve colour, shape, and fabric quality.

Hand wash step by step 1. Fill a basin with cool or lukewarm water (max 30°C).  2. Add a small amount of mild detergent and mix gently.  3. Submerge the garment and swirl softly — do not scrub.  4. Rinse thoroughly with cool water until no soap remains.  5. Gently press out excess water — never wring.  6. Lay flat or hang to dry in shade.
04 · Storage

Storing your
pieces properly.

How you store your clothes is just as important as how you wash them. Good storage keeps fabric fresh and shape intact.

🪝
Hang Dresses
Hang dresses and blouses on padded or wide hangers to maintain shoulder shape and prevent creasing.
📦
Fold Knits
Never hang knits — they stretch. Fold and store flat in a drawer or on a shelf to keep their shape.
💨
Breathable Bags
Store off-season pieces in breathable cotton garment bags — never plastic, which traps moisture.
🌿
Natural Repellent
Use cedar blocks or lavender sachets instead of mothballs. Keeps clothes fresh without chemical odour.
🌑
Away from Light
Store away from direct sunlight and artificial lighting. UV rays fade fabric colour over time.
💧
Keep Dry
Ensure garments are completely dry before storing. Damp clothes lead to mould and musty odours.
05 · Ironing

Ironing &
steaming.

A steamer is always preferable to an iron for Shenova pieces — it's gentler on delicate fabrics and removes wrinkles without direct contact.

Ironing tips Always check the care label first. Iron on the lowest setting suitable for the fabric. For embroidered or embellished pieces, always iron inside-out or use a pressing cloth between the iron and the garment. Never iron directly on prints, beads, or sequins.
